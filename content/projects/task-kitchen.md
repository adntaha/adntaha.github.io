A to-do list tells you *what*, never *when*, and the gap between the two is where procrastination lives. In Task Kitchen you add tasks with a rough duration, and the app lays them out as blocks of time across your day, fitted around what's already in your calendar. No dragging things into slots by hand.

It's one Flutter codebase, published on both the App Store and Google Play, with Firebase behind it. We registered the company in Québec, and I built the landing page from scratch for the launch. On Product Hunt it reached #13 Product of the Day with more than 130 upvotes. It made $50 in its first 48 hours and passed 500 downloads on Google Play.
