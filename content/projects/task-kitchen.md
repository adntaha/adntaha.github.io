A to-do list tells you *what*, never *when*, and the gap between the two is where procrastination lives. Task Kitchen takes the list and lays it out as blocks of time across your day, so the next thing to do is always obvious.

The main product is a cross-platform mobile app; there's also a web version, built at a hackathon. In its first year Task Kitchen made more than $500 in revenue, and on Product Hunt it reached #13 Product of the Day with more than 130 votes.
